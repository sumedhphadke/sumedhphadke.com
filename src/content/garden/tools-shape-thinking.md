The tool you reach for first changes the question you end up asking.
A spreadsheet makes you think in rows. A whiteboard makes you think
in clusters. A text file makes you think in sequences.

This is not a complaint — it is a design constraint. Choose the tool
that matches the shape of the problem, not the shape of your habit.
