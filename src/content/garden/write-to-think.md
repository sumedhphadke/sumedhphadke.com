Most people treat writing as the thing that happens after thinking.
But writing is where thinking actually occurs. The act of putting
words down forces you to resolve ambiguity that your brain was
happily ignoring.

If you are stuck on a problem, write about it. Not to explain it to
someone else — to explain it to yourself. The explanation is the work.
